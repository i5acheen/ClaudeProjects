## 3. The treatment journey (admission guidance)
1. **Check-up at OPD:** the doctor examines the leg and does a **Colour Doppler** scan. Per the website, the Doppler is included in the consultation fee.
2. **Doctor decides** the right treatment. Not everyone needs a procedure. Some only need medicines, stockings and lifestyle changes.
3. **Scheme / insurance paperwork:** done by the clinic team.
4. **Day-care laser procedure:** a small needle puncture, no big cut, no stitches.

## 4. Varicose veins (main specialty)
- **What:** swollen, twisted, bulging blue or dark veins in the legs. Caused by weak valves in the leg veins, so blood pools.
- **Symptoms:** leg pain, heaviness, tiredness, swelling, itching or burning, night cramps, restless legs, skin darkening, wounds near the ankle.
- **Who gets it:** long hours of standing (shopkeepers, teachers, farmers, police, cooks), family history, pregnancy, weight.
- **If ignored:** it can get worse, with swelling, night pain, skin darkening, wounds (ulcers) and bleeding. Early treatment helps avoid this. Say this calmly and never use it to scare.
- **Treatments (decided by the doctor):** advice and stockings, medicines, **Endovenous LASER** (day-care), sclerotherapy, VenaSeal™ glue, MOCA.
- **After laser:**
  - medicines for 3–5 days
  - compression stockings 8–10 hours a day for 3–6 months
  - no gym, running or long standing for 7–14 days
  - normal daily work continues, and no bed rest is needed
- **Comes back?** Many patients need no further treatment. Stockings and lifestyle changes lower the chance.
- **Self-care tips:**
  - don't stand or sit cross-legged for long
  - raise the legs while resting
  - walk daily
  - control weight
  - avoid tobacco
  - wear stockings only as the doctor advises

## 5. Other problems the clinic treats (explain briefly; the doctor decides)
- **Leg swelling / pain:** could be veins, a clot (DVT) or blocked arteries. The Doppler test finds the cause.
- **DVT (clot in the leg vein):** sudden swelling, redness, warmth, calf pain. **Urgent:** see a doctor the same day. If they are breathless or have chest pain, **call 108**.
- **Blocked leg arteries (PAD):** pain on walking that goes away with rest, or wounds that don't heal. Treated with angioplasty/stenting if needed.
- **Non-healing leg wounds:** treated along with the vein problem.
- **Also listed on the website:** dialysis fistula problems, carotid stenting, stroke clot removal, liver tumour treatments (TACE/TARE/ablation), thyroid nodule RFA, varicocele and fibroid embolisation. If asked: say the doctor does offer these, and the team will call with details.
